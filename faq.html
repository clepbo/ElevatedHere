Privacy

What can and cannot be seen

Money

Funding, fees and unspent budget

By card or by transfer to a dedicated virtual account in your organisation’s name. Card top-ups carry the gateway fee, VAT on that fee and the electronic transfer levy — all itemised before you confirm, and charged on top so the full amount reaches the wallet. Bank transfer carries no processing fee.

It stays yours. Allowances are caps, not commitments — money only leaves the wallet when a beneficiary books a service. Unencumbered funds can be withdrawn to your verified organisation account, subject to a hold on larger amounts as an anti-money-laundering control.

Funds move into escrow when a session is booked, leaving your budget at that point, and are released once delivery is confirmed. You see every stage on the transaction itself, along with gross fee, platform fee and net payout.

Yes. A restricted source can only fund the programmes named in the award, every resulting debit is tagged to that funder, and the grant is tracked against its own period rather than your financial year.

Changing your organisation currency does not convert what you already hold. A second balance opens in the new currency, both remain spendable, and converting between them is an explicit action with the rate and timestamp stamped onto the transaction.
