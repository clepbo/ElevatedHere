Paying for it

Four ways a session gets paid for

Cost is the reason most people never book. There is more than one route to a session, and the platform treats them all the same once you are in it.

You pay

Top up a wallet or take a subscription, in your own currency, by card, transfer or mobile money. Session-by-session or a package.

Your employer pays

An allowance from a workplace wellness programme. They see that it was used, never what was said.

A programme pays

An NGO, a government ministry or a funder covers a cohort. You claim your place and book like anyone else.

Insurance pays

Check eligibility, submit a claim or get reimbursed, with insurers integrated directly where cover exists.
