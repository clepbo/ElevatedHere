The hard part

Your people will only use it if they trust it.

A sponsored benefit nobody touches is not a saving — it is a failed programme. The biggest driver of uptake is certainty that the organisation paying cannot read what was said.

What you see

That a beneficiary enrolled, activated, booked and attended, and what it cost. Aggregate outcome trends across cohorts of five or more.

What you never see

Session content, notes, recordings, case evidence, diagnoses. Not a locked row you could request access to — it is structurally absent.
