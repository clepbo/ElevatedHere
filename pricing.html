On top of seats

The only other charge

Platform fee — 5% of service spend

Shown on every transaction: gross fee, platform fee, net to provider. On ₦10,000,000 of sessions that is ₦500,000, and ₦9,500,000 reaches practitioners.

Payment processing — at cost

Card top-ups carry the gateway fee (around 1.5%, capped) plus VAT and the electronic transfer levy, itemised before you confirm. Bank transfer is free.

Unused allowance is never charged. Money only leaves the wallet when a beneficiary actually books a service.
