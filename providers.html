Getting paid

Escrow, not invoices

The organisation’s money moves into escrow when a session is booked. When delivery is confirmed, it is released to you.

Funded before you start

You can see the budget exists and is committed before the first appointment. No chasing, no bad debt.

Transparent fees

Gross fee, platform fee and net payout shown on every engagement. Nothing deducted you did not see first.

Confidentiality intact

You report that a session happened. You never report what happened in it — not to the sponsor, not to us.
