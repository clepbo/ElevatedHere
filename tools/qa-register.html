D2

ROI definition

Phase 1 reports return in outcomes, not naira. A verified outcome is a rise of at least one minimal important difference (8 points) on the seven-dimension index, with baseline and endpoint both present. Monetised ROI is designed and withheld until absence and attrition baselines are supplied.

Approval thresholds

Banded by amount or share of pool, whichever puts the request higher. Owner above 2,000,000 or 20%; dual Owner approval above 10,000,000; any cap, billing or restricted-fund change is Owner at any value. Two business days, then escalation.

Naira glyph

Inter draws the two bars of the naira sign slightly wide, so they overlap the first digit and read as a strikethrough. Switching the display format to NGN 12.37M would remove it, at the cost of touching roughly 500 labels.
